⚡ Automated by Procedures Updater
1
Bi-Sprint Release Calls
Change management attends to track what's shipping.
→
2
Flag Relevant Procedures
Summarizes changes and flags which procedures they affect.
→
3
Update Procedure Doc
Procedure owner updates the doc in the internal procedures tool.
4
Submit for First Review
Submits a SharePoint form to the first-line reviewer (Small Business team).
→
5
First-Line Review
Reviewer checks the update — may kick it back with questions.
→
6
Route to Compliance
First-line reviewer emails the internal third-party compliance team.
7
Compliance Review
Third party reviews — may also kick back with questions or edits.
→
8
Publish
Third party owns and publishes the final procedure.
