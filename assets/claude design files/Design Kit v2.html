Deep blue carries the primary action and links. Green is the secondary action and system-OK. The light steps (--blue, --green) are large-fill colors — figures, panels, big type; anything filled with text at 14px or below uses the deep step, which clears AA on bone. Rust (#C2603F) is the only warm value in the system and is functional only: validation, warnings, errors. Never decorative, never a brand accent.
