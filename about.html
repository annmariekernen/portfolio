About me

My name is Ann Marie Kernen, and I'm currently a Product Designer at Freddie Mac on the myOptigo platform. Before this I was at Wells Fargo on Small Business Banking Technology, and before that a boutique design agency called Futuresight. At all three, I've mainly worked on B2B and enterprise web applications.

I'm originally from the Philly area — if you've ever seen Mare of Easttown, that was filmed in my hometown. I moved to DC for college, fell in love with the city, and have been here ever since.

I've been interested in digital design since I was a kid designing my Myspace profile, then Tumblr after that — apparently there's an entire generation of us.

If you've made it this far, thank you for taking the time to look through my portfolio.
